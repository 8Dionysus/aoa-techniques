# Adverse Effects Review

## Technique
- id: AOA-T-0077
- name: harvest-packet-contract

## Review focus
- promotion from `promoted` to `canonical` after live cross-context reinforcement from `aoa-sdk` checkpoint closeout and supporting public LangSmith reviewed-run curation docs
- confirm that `HARVEST_PACKET` remains one bounded reviewed-session packet nucleus, not memory canon, routing authority, dashboard schema, owner acceptance, or a replacement for later family seams

## Failure modes
- the packet becomes a recap blob instead of a container for bounded extract records
- optional fields start carrying hidden routing, diagnosis, repair, progression, quest, or promotion verdicts
- accepted candidates lack evidence refs, owner hints, next surfaces, or nearest-wrong-target posture
- local SDK path, hook, or command details are treated as invariant packet requirements
- downstream consumers mutate packet meaning instead of reading it as a handoff surface

## Negative effects
- an oversized packet can blur the boundary between donor extraction and later owner seams
- packet completeness can be mistaken for final owner acceptance or proof
- optional family fields can create hidden authority if they are not explicitly subordinate
- local storage layout can leak into the public technique as if it were the contract
- consumers may skip rereading reviewed evidence because the packet looks structured

## Misuse patterns
- treating the packet as a universal session database, memory object, or dashboard schema
- stuffing unresolved diagnosis, repair, progression, quest, or routing decisions into free-form packet fields
- adding every downstream family field before that family seam is stable
- emitting one packet before donor extraction has produced bounded extract records
- allowing a packet consumer to overwrite sibling-owner verdicts

## Detection signals
- reviewers cannot distinguish required packet spine from optional extensions
- `session_ref`, reviewed artifact refs, extract records, or evidence refs are missing
- optional fields read as accepted verdicts rather than pointers to later seams
- the packet grows substantially whenever a new downstream consumer appears
- owner follow-through starts from packet fields without carrying evidence refs forward

## Mitigations
- keep the required packet spine small and explicit
- require one bounded extract per reusable unit
- preserve optional family fields as adjunct pointers only
- keep later family seams explicit even when the packet links to them
- route memory, routing, diagnosis, repair, progression, quest, and owner acceptance to sibling techniques or owner repos

## Recommendation
- safe to promote as a canonical agent-workflow handoff technique when the packet remains a reviewed-session nucleus that downstream seams consume without absorbing their authority
- keep future revisions narrow: do not absorb memory canon, routing authority, owner acceptance, closeout dashboard behavior, diagnosis, repair, progression, quest promotion, stats refresh, or local SDK storage detail into this bundle
