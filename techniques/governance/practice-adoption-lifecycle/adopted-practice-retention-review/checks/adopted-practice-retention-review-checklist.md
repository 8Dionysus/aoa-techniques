# adopted-practice-retention-review checklist

Use this checklist before keeping an adopted or shadowed practice active.

- [ ] One adopted or shadowed practice is named.
- [ ] The original adoption or shadow-use record is visible.
- [ ] The owner and active behavior surface are named.
- [ ] Current evidence is compared against the original adoption reason.
- [ ] Drift, negative effects, owner discomfort, and support cost were checked.
- [ ] Rollback, quarantine, or obsolescence route is concrete enough to use.
- [ ] The verdict is one of `retain`, `revise`, `quarantine`, `defer`, or `route_to_obsolescence`.
- [ ] The verdict does not imply new adoption, deletion, proof, memory, skill, or runtime activation.
- [ ] The next review interval or reason to stop watching is explicit.
