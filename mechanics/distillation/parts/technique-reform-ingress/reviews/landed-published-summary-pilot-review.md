## What The Thirteenth Pilot Proved

- `proof/` can hold published-summary practice without pretending the path is
  proof verdict authority.
- A shelf can mix `artifact`, `lift`, `validation`, and `guardrail` when the
  browsing question is one operating corridor: store the latest summary,
  derive a bounded backlog, diagnose summary trust, and render required versus
  optional summary sources.
- Moving the shelf out of broad `evaluation/` did not erase `domain`
  frontmatter truth; the path now names the browseable proof-facing
  neighborhood, while `domain` still names the evaluation review lane.
- `AOA-T-0011` moving with the package made the consumer policy easier to find
  without collapsing it into a dashboard or package appendix.
- The previous `evaluation-chain` shelf plus this shelf now complete the
  current proof-side summary corridor: produce and promote summary evidence,
  then store, roll up, diagnose, and render published summaries.
- The shelf works for small-agent selection because each leaf remains one
  compact executable move rather than one mega-technique for published-summary
  platforms.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `AOA-T-0011` remains the live watch seam. It is reusable consumer policy, but
  future examples can still make it look package-specific if remediation and
  integrity examples crowd out the general required-versus-optional contract.
- `AOA-T-0010` can attract hard-gate language if downstream consumers begin to
  treat diagnostic `attention` as enforcement without a separate rollout.
- `AOA-T-0008` can drift into remediation execution or trust verdict language
  if bucket wording stops being a bounded backlog surface.
- `AOA-T-0006` can attract archive governance or retention policy if the
  latest-plus-history contract is widened into whole artifact lifecycle
  doctrine.
- The proof trunk now has three successful but authority-sensitive shelves, so
  later proof-side shelves should still receive landed review before another
  migration.
