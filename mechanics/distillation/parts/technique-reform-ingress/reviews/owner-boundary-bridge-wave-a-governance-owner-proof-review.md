## Cross-Owner Read

Wave A confirms the same split from the two pilots, but under heavier
pressure.

`aoa-techniques` owns the reusable move: make the choice, gate, readiness
classification, proof loop, owner landing, publish guard, or mirror parity
visible. It does not own the destination action.

Adjacent split:

- `Agents-of-Abyss` owns constitutional center doctrine and ecosystem-level
  owner law.
- `aoa-routing` owns route products and dispatch hints; fork cards and risk
  passports are advisory inputs only.
- `aoa-skills` owns accepted executable workflows, activation, commands, and
  package behavior.
- `aoa-evals` owns proof categories, empirical verdicts, and eval harnesses.
- `aoa-memo` owns memory objects, recall truth, and writeback semantics.
- `aoa-kag` owns derived graph or retrieval substrate meaning.
- `aoa-playbooks` owns recurring scenario choreography and campaign routes.
- `aoa-agents` owns role, progression, checkpoint, and recurrence contracts at
  the agent layer.
- runtime and local project owners own service lifecycle, job runners,
  deployment, approval execution, adoption, and rollback.

## Wave Findings

Decision-routing holds because each leaf is deliberately advisory. `AOA-T-0076`
chooses a primary owner shape for one isolated unit; `AOA-T-0078` keeps real
forks visible; `AOA-T-0079` adds small route posture. None decides final route
authority.

Approval-evidence holds because the two leaves separate one immediate
fail-closed boundary from one longer durable job seam. `AOA-T-0068` blocks
side effects on non-allow verdicts, while `AOA-T-0069` preserves approval
state across pause and resume. Neither becomes scheduler, policy, or runtime
ownership.

Automation-readiness holds because all three leaves stop before automation
execution. `AOA-T-0086` classifies fitness, `AOA-T-0087` names a first honest
landing, and `AOA-T-0088` records approval burden. The examples keep schedule,
seed-ready, and checkpoint language descriptive rather than sovereign.

Owner-truth-closeout holds because the five leaves keep proof and owner
anchors visible without becoming the owner. Ingress/guard posture, audit
proof, GitHub landing, generated-publish validation, and mirror parity remain
separate atoms rather than one closeout law.
