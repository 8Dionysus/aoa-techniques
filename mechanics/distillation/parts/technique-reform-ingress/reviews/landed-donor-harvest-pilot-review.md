## What The Nineteenth Pilot Proved

- `continuity/` can hold reviewed-session donor material without becoming a
  memory layer, session automation layer, or final promotion board.
- Progression and quest vocabulary can live in the shelf when it remains
  downstream of reviewed evidence and explicitly smaller than owner truth.
- Path placement can improve browsing while preserving `domain:
  agent-workflows`, current `kind` values, promoted status, and canonical
  readiness holds.
- The shelf gives small agents a useful local sequence: harvest candidates,
  preserve packet shape, lift progression evidence, then optionally add a
  narrative overlay.
- Generated projection rows converged on current paths after migration without
  becoming `tree_path` frontmatter truth.
