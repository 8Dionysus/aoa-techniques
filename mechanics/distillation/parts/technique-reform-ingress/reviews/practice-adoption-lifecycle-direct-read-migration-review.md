## Verdict

Accept `governance/practice-adoption-lifecycle` as the twenty-seventh bounded
migration pilot.

The shelf holds after direct reading because all three bundles govern one
local practice lifecycle without importing the whole Method-growth mechanic.
`AOA-T-0101` gates whether a shared pattern may become local behavior.
`AOA-T-0103` reviews whether an adopted or shadowed practice should remain
active after real use. `AOA-T-0104` routes a practice that should not remain
active as-is toward an owner receipt without deleting, deprecating, or erasing
evidence.

consent, deletion, deprecation execution, proof authority, memory truth, skill
activation, route mutation, runtime change, permanent practice retention,
canonical promotion, sibling owner acceptance, KAG promotion, ToS canon, or

## Why This Shelf Holds

- The common object is the lifecycle of one local practice, not automation
  readiness, promotion verdicting, or generic workflow execution.
- The three leaves form a compact review sequence: gate local adoption, review
  current retention after use, then route obsolescence pressure when retention
  is no longer honest.
- The shelf belongs under `governance` because the browsing question is which
  consent, retention, rollback, owner receipt, and stop-line must remain
  visible before local behavior changes.
- The shelf remains small-agent usable: choose one adoption gate, one
  retention assessment, or one obsolescence handoff packet, then stop.
- The Method-growth origin stays provenance and adaptation context; it does
  not become an imported mechanic contract inside the technique tree.

## Governance Trunk Fit

`practice-adoption-lifecycle` fits the governance trunk because these
techniques keep local adoption, retention, and obsolescence choices explicit
before an owner surface treats a practice as durable, active, superseded, or
ready for deprecation review. They should not stay buried inside broad
`agent-workflows` once the tree migration can preserve frontmatter truth:
all three can move under
`techniques/governance/practice-adoption-lifecycle/` while keeping current
`domain`, `kind`, and promoted status unchanged.
