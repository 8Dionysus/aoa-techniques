## What The Eleventh Pilot Proved

- The first `proof/` shelf can hold mixed historical domains when the browsing
  question is proof-facing support rather than frontmatter ownership.
- `AOA-T-0016` stayed a docs artifact: moving it did not make bounded context
  mapping a proof verdict or architecture taxonomy.
- `AOA-T-0015` and `AOA-T-0017` stayed evaluation validations: moving them did
  not create an eval-suite owner, test policy, or mandatory coverage doctrine.
- The shelf is useful for small agents because it gives one compact support
  triangle: name the seam, expose the consumer-visible contract, and constrain
  stable invariants.
- Broad `docs/` and broad `evaluation/` both shrank while preserving authored
  frontmatter truth.
- Generated projection helped route the move but remained weaker than the
  authored bundles, the route card, and the migration receipt.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `proof/` is a high-authority word. Future proof-side shelves need the same
  local restraint so path placement does not imply proof by location.
- `AOA-T-0016` can still attract generic DDD or architecture-program language.
  The local path is clearer, but the bundle must keep vocabulary mapping
  narrower than full design doctrine.
- `AOA-T-0015` can still attract large test-suite or hidden-internal contract
  pressure. It should remain consumer-visible boundary validation.
- `AOA-T-0017` can still attract generic property-testing glamour. It should
  keep invariant quality and bounded assumptions explicit.
- The next proof-side candidate, `evaluation-chain`, carries stronger gate and
  CI pressure than `skill-support`, so it needs direct reading before any
  migration.

adjacent to `skill-support`, but stronger: it produces summary contracts,

Why direct-read first:

The generated projection marks `evaluation-chain` as `candidate`, but
`AOA-T-0007` carries approval and irreversible-risk pressure. Direct reading
must decide whether the three leaves form one proof shelf or whether gate
promotion should wait because it can blur into release policy, CI ownership,
or eval authority.

Why not `published-summary`, `review-evidence`, or `owner-truth-closeout`
first:

`published-summary` is also proof-facing, but it is centered on published
surface storage, remediation, integrity, and rendering. `review-evidence` and
`owner-truth-closeout` are later, heavier shelves because they carry review
state, missing-evidence, claim challenge, release, and closeout proof pressure.
`evaluation-chain` is the nearer controlled test after `skill-support`.
