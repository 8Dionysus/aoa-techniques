## What The Ninth Pilot Proved

- `instruction/` can hold a cross-domain guardrail shelf without rewriting
  frontmatter ownership.
- A shelf can mix `docs` and `agent-workflows` when the shared browsing
  question is a boundary around capability-like objects rather than one owner
  lane.
- The three leaves stay distinct after moving: skill-command ownership,
  primary source priority, and recommendation/actionability did not collapse
  into one capability framework.
- Broad `docs/` and broad `agent-workflows/` both shrink without losing their
  frontmatter truth.
- Route-card economy still works. The useful bridge is a compact local shelf
  statement, not a heavy law block repeated through every moved bundle.
- Standalone portability is stronger after the move: external builders can
  reuse one capability-boundary guardrail without deploying OS Abyss or
  accepting AoA routing, registry, host-inventory, shell, or runtime doctrine.

  should take next.

Reason:

`skill-discovery` is the cleanest next review target because it checks the
remaining skill-adjacent pressure beside `capability-boundary`.
`AOA-T-0041` owns curated discoverability over upstream-owned skills without
claiming sync, installer, capability, or registry authority. `AOA-T-0042` owns
pre-surface upstream source availability and manifest-readiness without
becoming monitoring, registry governance, trust scoring, security scanning, or
routing policy. Together they ask one useful question: before a skill-like
object is surfaced for selection, what makes discovery reviewable without
claiming source ownership or execution authority?

Why direct-read first:

The generated projection marks both rows as `boundary-watch`, and that is
right. The shelf crosses `docs` and `evaluation`, touches external upstream
sources, and sits near marketplace language, registry language, security
language, installer behavior, and routing pressure. A direct-read review must
decide whether editorial discovery and pre-surface source readiness are one
bounded shelf before any path migration.

Why not proof, governance, runtime, or owner-closeout first:

Those shelves carry stronger verdict, approval, execution, or owner-truth
pressure. The tree should finish this skill-adjacent instruction-side read
before taking on proof, governance, runtime, closeout, or automation semantics.
