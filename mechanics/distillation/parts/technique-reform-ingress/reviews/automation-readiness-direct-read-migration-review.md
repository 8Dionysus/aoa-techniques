## Verdict

Accept `governance/automation-readiness` as the twenty-fifth bounded migration
pilot.

The shelf holds after direct reading because all three bundles answer the same
small governance question before any automation-facing lift: is this recurring
route ready enough to classify, where should it first land, and what approval
or rollback burden blocks a seed-ready claim? `AOA-T-0086` makes automation
desire evidence-backed through a small fit matrix. `AOA-T-0087` chooses the
first honest landing without turning that landing into implementation.
`AOA-T-0088` prevents hidden authority, mutation, rollback gaps, or self-change
pressure from pretending to be automation readiness.

The acceptance is narrow. It does not create automation policy authority, seed
canon, skill acceptance, skill activation, scheduler doctrine, hidden
automation governance, route mutation, memory write, runtime behavior, KAG
promotion, ToS canon, or broad orchestration governance. It does not canonically
promote these bundles, does not change their `domain`, `kind`, status,
evidence, relations, maturity, validation-strength metadata, or public-safety
posture, and does not move files from this review pack alone.

## Why This Shelf Holds

- The common object is automation-readiness judgment, not automation itself.
- The three leaves form a small before-action sequence: classify readiness,
  choose first landing, then raise checkpoint burden when readiness crosses
  authority or rollback boundaries.
- All three came from the automation-opportunity lineage but were already
  extracted as separate promoted techniques with public-safe examples,
  checklists, origin evidence, second-context adaptation, and canonical
  readiness holds.
- The shelf belongs under `governance` because the browsing question is how
  automation boundaries stay explicit before action, not how an agent executes
  the later automation.
- The shelf is small enough for small-agent retrieval: use one readiness
  matrix, one first-landing verdict, or one approval-sensitivity check, then
  stop.

## Governance Trunk Fit

`automation-readiness` fits the governance trunk because these techniques keep
choice and control posture visible before action. They should not stay buried
inside broad `agent-workflows` once the tree migration can preserve frontmatter
truth: all three can move under `techniques/governance/automation-readiness/`
while keeping `domain: agent-workflows`, `kind: assessment`, and promoted
status unchanged.
