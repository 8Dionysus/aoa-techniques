## Verdict

Accept `proof/review-evidence` as the twenty-second bounded migration pilot.

The shelf holds after direct reading because all three bundles carry the same
portable review-state object: one narrow evidence action that can pressure,
request, or supply support without turning the review into proof. `AOA-T-0107`
challenges one vulnerable locus inside one claim. `AOA-T-0105` asks for one
missing evidence object that could change or narrow the review. `AOA-T-0106`
offers one scoped evidence reference with relevance, support limit, and reliance
condition. Together they form a small proof-facing shelf for review evidence,
not proof verdict authority, not evaluation ownership, and not Agon move law.

The acceptance is narrow. It does not canonically promote these bundles, does
not change their `domain`, `kind`, status, evidence, relations, maturity, or
public-safety posture, and does not move files from this review pack alone.

## Why This Shelf Holds

- The common object is bounded review evidence, not proof result, evaluation
  result, or review-board process.
- The three leaves are a compact review sequence without becoming a chain:
  challenge one claim locus, request one missing object, or offer one available
  reference.
- Each leaf is usable by a small agent because it forces one object, one locus,
  or one reference instead of broad research or debate choreography.
- Each leaf carries origin evidence and canonical-readiness holds that preserve
  the same boundary: promoted public bundle, not canonical default and not Agon
  source mutation.
- The projection's `boundary-watch` marker is appropriate because the shelf
  touches proof language while repeatedly refusing proof verdict authority.

## Proof Trunk Fit

The projected `proof` trunk should hold techniques where the primary browsing
question is how validation, review evidence, summary integrity, or owner-truth
support remains inspectable without becoming the owning proof or eval system.

`review-evidence` fits that trunk because these bundles help a reviewer handle
evidence pressure honestly. They do not define proof verdict law, eval-suite
ownership, review-board workflow, Agon move law, actor eligibility, route
mutation, memory intake, runtime behavior, KAG promotion, ToS canon, or skill
activation. The path placement should improve tree browsing while preserving
the current `domain` and `kind` truth for all three leaves.

## Watch Signals

- `AOA-T-0107` can drift into adjudication if one-locus pressure starts being
  treated as proof that a claim is false.
- `AOA-T-0105` can drift into evidence theater if one missing object becomes a
  broad research assignment or a disguised failure verdict.
- `AOA-T-0106` can drift into proof-by-link or source-truth transfer if support
  limits and reliance conditions are omitted.
- All three leaves remain promoted, not canonical; canonical-readiness notes
  still ask for a second live context outside the Agon handoff lineage.
- Generated topology labels on capability, substrate, execution profile, and
  risk should remain scout evidence, not frontmatter truth.
