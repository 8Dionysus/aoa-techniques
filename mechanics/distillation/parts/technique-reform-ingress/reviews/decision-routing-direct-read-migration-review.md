not change their `domain`, `kind`, status, evidence, relations, maturity, or

## Why This Shelf Holds

- The common object is bounded decision legibility before action, not broad
  governance authority.
- The three leaves form a readable decision sequence: choose the owner shape,
  keep genuine route alternatives visible, and attach a small risk passport to
  explicit routes.
- Each leaf already refuses a stronger neighboring authority: routing systems,
  playbooks, context maps, promotion boards, approval systems, and policy
  engines.
- The shelf gives a small or orchestrated agent a clearer local neighborhood
  for choice support without requiring the whole AoA system.

## Governance Trunk Fit

The projected `governance` trunk should hold techniques where the primary
browsing question is how choices, approvals, control posture, and automation
boundaries stay explicit before action.

`decision-routing` fits that trunk only as local decision support. It does not
define the AoA constitution, replace `aoa-routing`, write role contracts, own
runtime dispatch, or issue approval policy. The path placement should improve
tree browsing while preserving `domain: agent-workflows` and the current
`kind: assessment` truth for all three leaves.

## Watch Signals

- `AOA-T-0076` can drift into repo taxonomy or context-map doctrine if it
  stops requiring one bounded candidate, one primary owner, and one nearest
  wrong target.
- `AOA-T-0078` can drift into hidden routing policy if a preferred branch is
  smuggled in as the only real route.
- `AOA-T-0079` can drift into approval or risk-scoring doctrine if posture
  fields are treated as authority rather than comparison help.
- `AOA-T-0078` and `AOA-T-0079` carry higher risk-posture scout signals than
  `AOA-T-0076`; migration should preserve their advisory wording and not
  create runtime dispatch semantics.
- All three remain promoted, not canonical; future canonical review still
  needs another independent downstream context.
