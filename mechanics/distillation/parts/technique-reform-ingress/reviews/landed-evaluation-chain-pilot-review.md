## What The Twelfth Pilot Proved

- `proof/` can hold a stronger evaluation-facing chain when the path is framed
  as evidence surface support rather than proof authority.
- A mixed `validation` / `guardrail` shelf can stay legible when the browsing
  question is sequence: produce a machine-readable summary, promote one signal
  cautiously, and publish a read-only context report.
- Moving the shelf out of broad `evaluation/` did not erase `domain`
  frontmatter truth; `domain` still names the historical review lane while the
  path names the browseable proof-facing neighborhood.
- `AOA-T-0032` staying promoted is a useful precedent: path placement is not
  maturity promotion.
- The shelf works for small-agent selection because each leaf remains one
  compact executable move rather than one combined CI gate package.
- The previous `skill-support` shelf and this `evaluation-chain` shelf now give
  the `proof/` trunk two different shapes: support primitives first, then an
  evidence-chain sequence.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `AOA-T-0007` is still the pressure point. Its `guardrail` role can attract
  release-policy or hidden-enforcement drift if future wording forgets the one
  narrow strict surface.
- `AOA-T-0003` can still be overused as if one summary contract were the whole
  proof substrate. Storage, history, rollup, and gate promotion must stay
  adjacent rather than folded into the producer.
- `AOA-T-0032` can still drift into generic observability, composition repair,
  or provider telemetry if the report starts prescribing fixes.
- The `proof/` trunk now contains enough authority pressure that every later
  proof-side shelf needs landed review before moving the next shelf.
- `published-summary` is close enough to the landed shelf to be the next
  direct-read target, but it carries storage, remediation, integrity, and
  rendering-policy seams that need a fresh migration review rather than reuse
  of old semantic and shadow reviews as path authority.

downstream neighbor of `evaluation-chain`. It lets the tree finish the current
