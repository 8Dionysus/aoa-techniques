## What The Sixteenth Pilot Proved

- `execution/` can start with a small shelf rather than the whole
  `agent-workflows` backbone.
- Path architecture can improve browsing without erasing frontmatter truth:
  the leaves still belong to `domain: agent-workflows`, but their browsing home
  now reflects execution readiness.
- The shelf can hold two graph-shaped leaves and one ladder-shaped leaf because
  the shared object is ready-work visibility, not graph modeling.
- `AOA-T-0055` remains useful beside the graph leaves only while its contract
  stays pre-execution: requirement -> design -> tasks before implementation,
  not methodology doctrine and not execution workflow.
- A new trunk route card can stay compact: it names the local shelf and stop
  lines without importing AoA constitutional law or project-local runtime
  authority.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `AOA-T-0055` remains the shelf watch line. If future examples make it more
  about methodology or template suites than ready task visibility, it may need
  a planning shelf review.
- `AOA-T-0049` and `AOA-T-0050` can still drift toward tracker product,
  graph-database, or project-management semantics if blocker truth stops being
  the center.
- The execution trunk now has one precedent, not a complete execution
  taxonomy. Neighboring shelves still need direct reading.
- `agent-workflows-core` remains too broad to move by adjacency. It should
  wait until smaller execution shelves prove the trunk shape.
- `runtime-truth-lifecycle` still carries runtime authority pressure and must
  not inherit readiness language from this shelf.

owns the artifact-first intent -> plan -> dry-run -> contract-check chain.

Why direct-read first:

The pair is stronger than `ready-work-graphs`: it carries dry-run safety,
contract verdicts, rollout fixtures, and CI or operator review surfaces. The
next review must confirm that this remains a portable execution technique
shelf rather than automation policy, API contract ownership, runtime dispatch,
or broad rollout governance.

`agent-workflows-core` is larger and more canonical; it should wait until
`execution/` has at least two smaller shelves with clear stop lines.
`runtime-truth-lifecycle` carries runtime, host, lifecycle, and service
authority pressure and should wait until the trunk has more non-runtime
precedent. `donor-harvest`, `decision-routing`, `automation-governance`,
`review-evidence`, and `owner-truth-closeout` still route through continuity,
governance, proof, or owner-acceptance pressure rather than intent-chain
execution.
