## Cross-Owner Read

Wave B keeps proof-support inside `aoa-techniques` while leaving proof
authority outside.

The local atom may produce, store, render, summarize, cite, request, or
challenge. It may not decide that correctness, adequacy, policy, owner
consent, release readiness, or actor authority is proven.

Adjacent split:

- `aoa-evals` owns eval bundles, categories, empirical proof, verdicts, and
  report authority.
- GitHub Actions or local CI owners own the workflow platform; techniques only
  describe portable report and summary shapes.
- runtime and storage owners own live remediation, monitoring, archive
  retention, object lifecycle, and operational repair.
- `aoa-routing`, `aoa-memo`, `aoa-kag`, `aoa-playbooks`, and `aoa-agents` own
  their own route, recall, graph, scenario, and role effects.
- Agon mechanics may be provenance for `AOA-T-0105` through `AOA-T-0107`, but
  ordinary technique bundles do not import Agon move law.

## Wave Findings

`evaluation-chain` holds because the chain is explicit but not sovereign.
`AOA-T-0003` produces the summary contract, `AOA-T-0007` promotes one signal
only through staged evidence, and `AOA-T-0032` reports on composition health
without doing composition or remediation.

`published-summary` holds because every leaf stays read-only or storage-policy
bounded. Latest/history storage, remediation snapshots, integrity snapshots,
and required/optional rendering are adjacent consumer surfaces, not one
generated-truth platform.

`review-evidence` holds because the three Agon-origin leaves are already
narrowed into ordinary review atoms. They ask for one object, offer one
reference, or challenge one locus. None issues a verdict or mutates owner
state.
