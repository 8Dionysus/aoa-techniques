## Verdict

The `governance/practice-adoption-lifecycle` shelf holds as the second
owner-boundary bridge pilot.

This pilot exercises a different boundary shape than `promotion-boundary`.
Instead of deciding a destination owner target, these leaves guard a local
practice's lifecycle before behavior becomes durable, remains active, or routes
toward obsolescence. `AOA-T-0101` gates one shared pattern before local
adoption. `AOA-T-0103` reviews one adopted or shadowed practice before active
retention continues. `AOA-T-0104` emits one obsolescence route packet without
performing deletion, deprecation, or erasure.

No source repair is accepted. The bundle contracts already keep Method-growth,
local owner consent, retention evidence, deprecation execution, proof, memory,
skill, routing, runtime, and sibling-owner authority outside the technique
atom.

## Cross-Owner Read

`aoa-techniques` owns the reusable practice atom and its public-safe stop-lines.
Method-growth owns technique-side movement grammar around adoption, retention,
obsolescence, and technique-to-skill handoff, but that mechanics route remains
provenance and context for these three leaves. It does not become bundle law.

Adjacent owner split:

- `Agents-of-Abyss` owns center Method-growth law, ecosystem identity, and
  owner-request grammar.
- `aoa-skills` owns accepted skill workflow, trigger contract, packaging,
  validation, and activation discipline.
- `aoa-playbooks` owns scenario composition and recurring route choreography.
- `aoa-evals` owns bounded proof claims and verdict shapes.
- `aoa-memo` owns memory-object structure, writeback, recall, and temporal
  relevance.
- `aoa-agents` owns role contracts, progression, recurrence, and checkpoint
  contract surfaces at the agent layer.
- `aoa-routing` owns advisory navigation and dispatch hints, not activation or
  source meaning.
- `aoa-kag` owns derived provenance-aware knowledge substrates, not authored
  technique meaning.

This pilot does not need source edits because each bundle already names its
local object, output shape, and stop condition before sibling authority would
begin.

## Repair Gate

No source repair is accepted.

Held, not defects:

| hold | reason |
|---|---|
| Method-growth vocabulary | the terms are provenance and adaptation context, while technique contracts stay atomic |
| local owner consent | the techniques require consent visibility but do not create consent |
| retention watch | retention is review posture, not permanent active status |
| obsolescence labels | route labels are owner-review packets, not deletion or deprecation execution |
| downstream hints | memory, stats, proof, skill, routing, and runtime consequences remain hints until accepted by their owners |
| empirical proof | no local small model executed these techniques in this pilot |

## Long-Pass Readiness

Together with the `promotion-boundary` pilot, this closes enough calibration to
start an owner-boundary bridge long pass.

The two pilots cover two different boundary families:

- destination-owner pressure: verdict, nearest-wrong rejection, skill-proposal
  handoff;
- local-practice lifecycle pressure: adoption gate, retention review,
  obsolescence route.

The long pass should now move shelf by shelf. It should repair source only when
the bundle text itself smuggles destination authority, hides the owner stop-line,
turns a bridge into implementation, or leaves a small agent unable to stop.
Otherwise, it should record `holds; no repair` and keep moving.

review packets. It does not publish credentials, private hosts, internal

repo implementation detail. Owner names are boundary markers, not claims that
