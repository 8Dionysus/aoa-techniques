## Findings

### Boundary Watch Is The Main Shape Of Wave B

Unlike Wave A, Wave B is intentionally made of surfaces that touch proof,
continuity, and governance pressure. The trunk route cards already say these
techniques must not become proof verdict law, memory policy, routing authority,
automation governance, approval policy, or AoA constitutional direction.

Direct reading confirms that the bundles keep their local technique shape:
each one names a small move, a bounded output, and a validation check that can
be inspected without granting the bundle stronger owner authority.

### Continuity Is Not Memory Or Playbook Authority

The handoff, compaction, donor, progression, and quest-adjacent bundles stay
usable because they preserve one reviewed state boundary. They do not claim to
own memory truth, playbook doctrine, progression authority, or final quest
verdicts. That separation is the reason `owner-boundary-watch` is healthy here.

### Governance Is Advisory Until An Owner Accepts It

Decision cards, passports, approval gates, automation matrices, promotion
reviews, and adoption lifecycle packets are technique-level moves only while
they expose posture before action. The reviewed bundles preserve this limit by
naming rejected targets, stop conditions, owner receipt, or explicit handoff
surfaces.

### Capsule Gap Appears In Owner-Truth Closeout

`AOA-T-0095` and `AOA-T-0096` are coherent in authored source, but the generated
capsule shorts lose readability around wrapped source bullets:

- `AOA-T-0095` capsule validation includes `before the;`
- `AOA-T-0096` capsule risk ends with `older or.`

This should not trigger broad bundle rewriting. The follow-through should first
decide whether the repair belongs to source line shaping, capsule extraction,
or both.

### No Leaf Repair Cohort From Wave B Alone

Wave B does not justify editing technique leaves yet. It does create one
generated-reader follow-through candidate pair. The first broader repair cohort
should still wait for Wave C plus corpus-wide synthesis.
