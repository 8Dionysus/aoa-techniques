## What The Tenth Pilot Proved

- `instruction/` can hold a very small boundary-watch shelf when the route card
  stays compact and the leaves remain distinct.
- A shelf can mix `docs` and `evaluation` when the shared browsing question is
  instruction-side surfacing rather than frontmatter ownership.
- Editorial discovery and upstream source-readiness stayed separate: curation
  did not become health checking, and readiness did not become marketplace
  grouping.
- Broad `docs/` and broad `evaluation/` both shrink without losing their
  frontmatter truth.
- Generated projection can guide migration order while remaining weaker than
  authored bundle meaning and migration receipts.
- Standalone portability improved: external builders can browse skill
  discovery techniques without importing OS Abyss installer, registry,
  routing, security, monitoring, or runtime doctrine.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `skill-discovery` remains a two-bundle shelf. It is useful and clear, but it
  should not be treated as a complete skill ecosystem taxonomy.
- Both bundles still touch external source pressure. Future edits must keep
  source-readiness separate from trust scoring, security scanning, supply-chain
  review, and generic monitoring.
- `AOA-T-0041` uses marketplace language. The path is clearer, but future
  readers still need the local curation boundary to stay visible.
- `AOA-T-0042` uses health language. The path is clearer, but the verdict must
  stay pre-surface readiness rather than uptime or registry governance.
- The next proof-side shelves carry stronger authority pressure than the
  instruction-side sequence just completed.

Reason:

`skill-support` is the cleanest next review target because the family scout
marks it as a stable shelf candidate, all three bundles are canonical, and the
cluster gives small agents a practical support triangle: name the boundary,
test the contract, and constrain stable invariants. It is also the right first
proof-side pressure because it is smaller and less owner-loaded than
`owner-truth-closeout`, less runtime-loaded than `runtime-truth-lifecycle`, and
less governance-loaded than `automation-governance`.

Why direct-read first:

The projected trunk is `proof`, but the three techniques are support moves, not
proof authority. `AOA-T-0016` is a docs artifact for semantic boundaries,
`AOA-T-0015` is boundary-contract validation, and `AOA-T-0017` is
invariant-oriented validation. Direct reading must decide whether
`techniques/proof/skill-support/` is clearer than leaving one leaf in `docs/`
and two leaves in `evaluation/`, while keeping proof verdicts and eval-owner
authority out of the path move.

Why not runtime, governance, owner-closeout, or review-evidence first:

`runtime-truth-lifecycle` crosses execution and runtime posture.
`automation-governance` is already marked split-pressure. `owner-truth-closeout`
touches publication, proof, and governance. `review-evidence` is promising, but
it is newer and closer to proof/verdict overclaim. `skill-support` is older,
canonical, compact, and foundational enough to test a proof-side trunk without
starting with the heaviest authority surface.
