## What The Twenty-Second Pilot Proved

- `proof/` can hold review-evidence atoms without making review evidence into
  proof verdict authority, eval ownership, review-board workflow, Agon law, or
  source-truth transfer.
- The shelf improves browsing because claim pressure, missing-evidence
  requests, and scoped references now sit near validation and summary
  integrity instead of being split across broad `agent-workflows` and `docs`
  homes.
- Path placement can preserve `domain: agent-workflows`, `domain: docs`,
  distinct `kind` values, promoted status, support files, and canonical
  readiness holds.
- The shelf gives small agents a compact local route: pressure one claim
  locus, request one missing object, or offer one scoped reference, then stop
  below proof.
- Generated projection rows converged on current paths after migration without
  becoming `tree_path` frontmatter truth.

## Remaining Weaknesses

- `AOA-T-0107` can still drift into adjudication if claim pressure is treated
  as proof that the claim is false.
- `AOA-T-0105` can still drift into evidence theater if one missing object
  becomes a broad research assignment or a disguised failure verdict.
- `AOA-T-0106` can still drift into proof-by-link if support limits and
  reliance conditions are omitted.
- The three leaves can still collapse into a vague review mini-workflow if
  challenge, request, and reference are not kept as separate one-use atoms.
- All three leaves remain promoted rather than canonical; their readiness notes
  still ask for another independent downstream context before default-use
  promotion.
- Future relation work should improve cross-shelf browsing without adding
  `tree_path`, `family`, capability, substrate, execution-profile, or risk
  frontmatter.
