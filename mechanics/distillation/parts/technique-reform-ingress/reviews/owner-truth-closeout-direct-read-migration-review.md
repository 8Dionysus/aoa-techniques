## Verdict

Accept `proof/owner-truth-closeout` as the twenty-fourth bounded migration
pilot.

The shelf holds after direct reading because all five bundles protect the same
portable proof-facing object: a route, mirror, publish, or closeout claim must
land against named owner truth instead of operator memory, staging residue,
local green confidence, or convenient nearby mirrors. `AOA-T-0091` makes
workspace ingress and mutation guard posture explicit before risky work.
`AOA-T-0092` closes reviewed findings only through live confirmation,
owner-surface repair, targeted proof, and owner-level closeout. `AOA-T-0095`
keeps a remote-only owner endcap GitHub-native, then rebinds coordination
truth to merged owner anchors. `AOA-T-0096` keeps generated publish confidence
honest against the workflow-pinned validation matrix. `AOA-T-0094` keeps one
canonical contract owner and allows mirrors only through parity validation.

The acceptance is narrow. It does not make `proof/owner-truth-closeout` AoA
constitutional law, root `AGENTS.md` law, workspace install doctrine,
public-share approval policy, GitHub platform policy, release governance,
cross-repo mirror co-ownership, skill activation, checkpoint automation, or
closeout automation. It does not canonically promote these bundles, does not
change their `domain`, `kind`, status, evidence, relations, maturity, or
public-safety posture, and does not move files from this review pack alone.

## Why This Shelf Holds

- The common object is owner-truth closure: do not let a route, publish,
  mirror, or remediation claim outrun its owning source and proof surface.
- The shelf is proof-facing because each leaf asks what evidence lets a route
  proceed or close honestly, not because the path itself proves correctness.
- The five leaves are adjacent around owner-boundary endings without forming
  one mandated closeout workflow: ingress and guard posture, finding proof,
  GitHub owner landing, generated-publish validation, and mirror parity are
  separate atoms.
- The projection's `boundary-watch` marker is appropriate because this shelf
  touches root guidance, public-share, GitHub, generated publish, mirrors, and
  owner law while repeatedly refusing to become those authorities.
- The shelf improves browsing because owner-truth checks currently split
  across broad `agent-workflows` and `docs` homes would be discoverable beside
  review evidence, evaluation chains, published summaries, and skill-support
  proof-side support.

## Proof Trunk Fit

The projected `proof` trunk should hold techniques whose primary browsing
question is what review, validation, evidence, summary integrity, or owner
truth check keeps a reusable move honest.

`owner-truth-closeout` fits that trunk because these bundles do not primarily
describe how to execute a work slice. They describe the evidence and ownership
conditions that make an entry, fix, publish, remote landing, or mirror claim
honest enough to proceed or close. The path placement should improve tree
browsing while preserving current `domain` and `kind` truth for all five
leaves.
