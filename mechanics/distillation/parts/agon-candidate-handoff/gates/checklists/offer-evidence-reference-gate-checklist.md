## Pass Conditions

- The move offers exactly one evidence reference.
- The reviewed claim or decision point is named before the reference.
- The reference is concrete enough to inspect, quote, rerun, or reject.
- The relevance statement explains why the reference matters to the claim.
- The scope statement says what the reference can support.
- The limit statement says what the reference cannot support.
- The review condition says how the reference may be used before reliance.
- The wording avoids proof-by-link, source-truth laundering, and citation
  theater.
- The output remains portable outside OS Abyss when an orchestrator supplies
  the claim, review state, and acceptable reference form.

## Fail Conditions

- The move offers a bundle of references instead of one scoped reference.
- The reference is vague, inaccessible, or not tied to the reviewed claim.
- The move treats the reference as a proof verdict.
- The move imports source truth, canon authority, memory writeback, routing, or
  KAG promotion.
- The move starts a workflow, route decision, arena action, or owner escalation.
- The move depends on Agon law to be understandable.

## Stop Lines

- Do not define Agon evidence move law.
- Do not issue proof, verdict, route, memory, rank, scar, KAG, ToS, runtime, or
  skill effects.
- Do not promote the candidate from this checklist.
- Do not draft a technique bundle until the evidence note and bundle-local
  review route agree that the atom stays compact.
