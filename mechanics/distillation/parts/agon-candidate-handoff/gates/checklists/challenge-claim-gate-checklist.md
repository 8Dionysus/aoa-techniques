## Pass Conditions

- The move challenges exactly one target claim.
- The challenged locus inside the claim is named.
- The pressure reason explains why that locus is vulnerable.
- The next evidence, scope, or revision question is named.
- The stop condition says the challenge is not a proof verdict.
- The wording avoids tone-as-evidence, personal attack, and broad debate.
- The output remains portable outside OS Abyss when an orchestrator supplies
  the claim, review state, and acceptable next-support form.

## Fail Conditions

- The move challenges a broad topic, actor, repo, or worldview instead of one
  claim.
- The challenged locus is vague or missing.
- The move decides the claim is false without proof.
- The move asks for broad research rather than one next support question.
- The move imports eval verdicts, route decisions, memory writeback, rank,
  scar, KAG promotion, ToS canon, or Agon law.
- The move depends on Agon law to be understandable.

## Stop Lines

- Do not define Agon stance move law.
- Do not issue proof, verdict, route, memory, rank, scar, KAG, ToS, runtime, or
  skill effects.
- Do not promote the candidate from this checklist.
- Do not draft a technique bundle until a readiness review chooses a
  registry-backed kind.
