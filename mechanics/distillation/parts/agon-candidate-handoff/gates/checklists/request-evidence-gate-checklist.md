## Pass Conditions

- The move asks for exactly one missing evidence object.
- The reviewed claim or decision point is named before the request.
- The requested object is concrete enough to be supplied or declared absent.
- The reason explains how the object would change, narrow, or reopen the review
  state.
- The return condition says what happens if the object appears.
- The return condition says what happens if the object remains unavailable.
- The wording avoids broad research, open-ended investigation, and general
  suspicion.
- The output remains portable outside OS Abyss when an orchestrator supplies
  the claim, review state, and acceptable evidence format.

## Fail Conditions

- The request asks for every possible source instead of one object.
- The move judges whether the claim is true.
- The move treats missing evidence as proof of failure.
- The move starts a workflow, route decision, memory write, arena action, or
  owner escalation.
- The move depends on Agon law to be understandable.

## Stop Lines

- Do not define Agon evidence move law.
- Do not issue proof, verdict, route, memory, rank, scar, KAG, ToS, runtime, or
  skill effects.
- Do not promote the candidate from this checklist.
- Do not draft a technique bundle until the evidence note and bundle-local
  review route agree that the atom stays compact.
