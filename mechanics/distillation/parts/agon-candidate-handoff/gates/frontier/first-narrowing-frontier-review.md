## Topology Read

The remaining frontier is not blocked because the ideas are weak. It is blocked
because most of the labels are already future topology cues rather than current
frontmatter truth.

- `primary_kind` is registry-backed for only `deny_closure`.
- The other seven candidates need a gate card that maps their cue to one current
  `kind` without adding a new kind too early.
- `capability_class`, `substrate`, `execution_profile`, and `risk_posture`
  should stay visible in the generated lens because they are the useful material
  for the coming classification reform.
- The next honest work is still one candidate at a time.
