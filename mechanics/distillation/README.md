# Distillation

This package owns the `aoa-techniques` side of the cross-project Distillation
mechanic: turning donor, cross-layer, legacy, or long-gap material into active
technique-shaped candidates without losing provenance or claiming canon too
early.

- [Donor Refinery Rubric](DONOR_REFINERY_RUBRIC.md): compact extraction law for
  turning donor material into reusable practice without copying residue.
- [External Import Runbook](EXTERNAL_IMPORT_RUNBOOK.md): maintainer-facing path
  from donor triage to draft, review, and merge.
- [External Technique Candidates](EXTERNAL_TECHNIQUE_CANDIDATES.md): public-safe
  external candidate inventory.
- [Cross-Layer Technique Candidates](CROSS_LAYER_TECHNIQUE_CANDIDATES.md):
  candidate capture across AoA sibling repos.
- [Long-Gap Canon Design](LONG_GAP_CANON_DESIGN.md): design posture for older
  practice material that needs careful re-entry.

Distillation may produce a candidate, intake route, or overlap hold. It does
not by itself promote a technique bundle or replace bundle-local evidence.
