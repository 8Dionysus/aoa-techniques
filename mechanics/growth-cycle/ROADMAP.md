# Growth Cycle Roadmap

This roadmap names Growth-cycle future pressure for `aoa-techniques`. It is not
a release commitment, source ledger, hidden scheduler, progression score, or
proof surface.

## Current Contour

Growth-cycle is the local route for mastery harvest, feat reflection, questbook
integration, reviewed closeout incubation, and stage-shaped technique pressure.

The package can expose repeated movement around canon hardening, but it cannot
claim final harvest, progression, quest closure, memory, proof, runtime, stats,
or playbook authority.

## Next Work

1. Decide whether `promotion-readiness-incubation` has repeated enough across
   reviewed routes to extract a real technique bundle. Use
   `stage-technique-anchors` first so the candidate stays distinct from donor
   harvest, owner placement, quest promotion, and progression-lift anchors.
2. Check whether feat-card reader surfaces need a generated manifest or should
   remain examples until stronger evidence exists.
3. Reconcile quest owner surfaces after generated quest catalog rebuilds and
   keep quest promotion distinct from technique canon promotion.
4. Preserve any discovered raw wave/source material in `legacy/raw/` and keep
   `legacy/INDEX.md`, `legacy/DISTILLATION_LOG.md`, and `PROVENANCE.md`
   aligned.

## When Time Comes

- Add a stage ID or stage card only after more than one local route needs the
  same reviewed Growth-cycle vocabulary.
- Promote a Growth-cycle-shaped practice into `techniques/` only when it is one
  portable move rather than the whole cycle.
- Route choreography to `aoa-playbooks`, proof to `aoa-evals`, memory to
  `aoa-memo`, and derived visibility to `aoa-stats`.

## Out Of Scope

- Hidden scheduler behavior.
- Autonomous repair or progression claims.
- Universal progression scores.
- Quest closure or playbook choreography.
- Memory canon, proof verdicts, runtime activation, or stats truth.
- Automatic technique promotion from feat or closeout language.
