# Audit Direction

Audit keeps promotion readiness and external evidence work reviewable without
turning queue pressure into canonical status.

## Current intent

- keep the promoted corpus queue legible
- keep evidence-sprint execution bounded to searched lanes and exact-fit
  reinforcement
- preserve closure precedents without making them proof authority for other
  bundles
- route donor intake and new extraction back to Distillation

## Boundaries

- bundle-local `TECHNIQUE.md` and notes own technique meaning and canonical
  readiness verdicts
- `generated/` surfaces summarize status and evidence; they do not authorize
  promotion by themselves
- Audit can name blockers and route work, but it does not silently flip
  technique status
- Queue links should point back to exact bundle evidence, external evidence, or
  generated summaries without becoming proof doctrine or final promotion
  authority.
