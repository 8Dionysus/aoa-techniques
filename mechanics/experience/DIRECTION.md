# Experience Direction

## Current Intent

Experience in `aoa-techniques` is the reusable-practice surface for governance,
authority, scope, handoff, service clarity, appeal, and sealed decision
patterns that emerge from the cross-project Experience mechanic.

The mechanic receives Experience pressure, but keeps the local output bounded:

- reusable practice notes
- owner-boundary reminders
- governance precedent surfaces
- appeal or decision reasoning surfaces
- office/service clarity contours

## Current Route

1. Use [Governance Precedent](parts/governance-precedent/README.md) when the
   question is how to document reusable governance practice.
2. Use [Authority Resolution](parts/authority-resolution/README.md) when actor
   capability and governance authority must stay separate.
3. Use [Appeal Reasoning](parts/appeal-reasoning/README.md) when a decision path
   needs reviewable appeal or overturn reasoning.
4. Use [Sealed Decision](parts/sealed-decision/README.md) when commit/reveal,
   hash-chain review, or tamper-detection practice is the reusable object.
5. Use [Scope Boundary](parts/scope-boundary/README.md),
   [Handoff Compression](parts/handoff-compression/README.md), and
   [Service Clarity](parts/service-clarity/README.md) for office/service-shaped
   practice that must stay below runtime and release authority.

## Boundaries

Experience does not mint technique canon by itself. A promoted or canonical
technique still needs bundle-local evidence and validation expected by
`aoa-techniques`.

Experience also does not activate live office or runtime behavior. Runtime
truth stays with `abyss-stack`, proof with `aoa-evals`, executable workflows with
`aoa-skills`, role posture with `aoa-agents`, scenario choreography with
`aoa-playbooks`, and Tree-of-Sophia meaning with `Tree-of-Sophia`.

## Current Structural Posture

The first active split moved seven formerly flat Experience files into
part-local homes. Their seed wording was preserved as active mechanics behavior,
not promoted into technique bundles.

The next work should decide one part at a time whether the reusable practice is
ready for `techniques/` or should remain a mechanics-only owner-boundary note.
