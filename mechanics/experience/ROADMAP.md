# Experience Roadmap

This roadmap names Experience future pressure for `aoa-techniques`. It is not a
release commitment, source ledger, office runtime, service contract, or proof
surface.

## Current Contour

Experience is a local candidate bridge for authority, sealed decisions,
governance precedent, appeal handling, handoff compression, scope boundaries,
and service clarity when those pressures may become one portable technique.

The package remains below AoA center Experience law and below owner-local
office, service, runtime, role, proof, and release authority. Its job is to
preserve reviewed pressure and reject broad drafts until one compact technique
atom is visible.

## Next Work

1. Review `authority-resolution` through the candidate bridge and decide whether
   a `capability-authority-separation-check` atom is distinct from owner-layer
   placement, host-actionability, and role contracts owned by `aoa-agents`.
2. Review `sealed-decision` through the candidate bridge and decide whether a
   sealed evidence packet can stay smaller than proof verdicts and general
   decision-rationale recording.
3. Split governance precedent from appeal handling before either lane becomes a
   technique draft.
4. Keep handoff compression, scope boundary, and service clarity in
   `hold_overlap` until their portable atom is smaller than live office,
   release, service runtime, and existing handoff/lifecycle bundles.
5. Preserve any discovered real wave/source material in `legacy/raw/` and keep
   `legacy/INDEX.md`, `legacy/DISTILLATION_LOG.md`, and `PROVENANCE.md`
   aligned.

## When Time Comes

- Add a new part only when a repeated Experience pressure no longer fits the
  current candidate bridge.
- Add examples only from reviewed local evidence that can stay public-safe and
  portable.
- Promote one Experience-shaped practice into `techniques/` only after it can
  name the atom, likely topology, owner boundary, examples, and validation.
- Route office, service, runtime, role, proof, release, or adoption truth to the
  stronger owner instead of localizing it here.

## Out Of Scope

- Live workspace runtime activation.
- Owner-local office installation, service authority, or release approval.
- Hidden memory, KAG, ToS, assistant, role, proof, or runtime authority.
- Importing AoA Experience law as local technique-canon law.
- Automatic technique promotion from Experience-shaped pressure.
