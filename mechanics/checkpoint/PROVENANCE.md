for `aoa-sdk`, `aoa-skills`, `aoa-agents`, `aoa-memo`, `aoa-playbooks`,
`aoa-evals`, `aoa-routing`, `aoa-stats`, `Dionysus`, and `abyss-stack`.

owner-request queue. Checkpoint pressure here is therefore local
