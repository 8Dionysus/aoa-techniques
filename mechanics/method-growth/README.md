# Method-Growth

This package owns the `aoa-techniques` side of the cross-project Method-growth
mechanic: moving repeated practice toward explicit technique adoption, owner
landing, retention, pruning, or technique-to-skill handoff without confusing
practice meaning with execution authority.

Start with:

- [Direction](DIRECTION.md): current intent, boundaries, and route posture.
- [Parts](PARTS.md): active part map.
- [Provenance](PROVENANCE.md): active-first bridge back to the pre-split v0.7
  adoption surfaces.
- [Landing Log](LANDING_LOG.md): dated accounting for structural landings.
- [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md): next honest passes.

Current active parts:

- [Technique To Skill Handoff](parts/technique-to-skill-handoff/README.md):
  handoff posture between reusable technique canon and bounded skill workflows.
- [Adoption Boundaries](parts/adoption-boundaries/README.md): owner stop-lines
  for adopting technique patterns.
- [Pattern Adoption](parts/pattern-adoption/README.md): reusable adoption
  precedent mechanics.
- [Retention Checks](parts/retention-checks/README.md): checks for whether an
  adopted pattern should remain active.
- [Obsolescence](parts/obsolescence/README.md): bounded removal and deprecation
  mechanics.

Method-growth can route a practice toward adoption or handoff. It does not make
another owner accept the practice, and it does not turn a technique into a skill.

## Method-Growth Gate

Before a Method-growth surface becomes a technique bundle, the active part
should be able to name:

- the reusable practice being promoted
- the owner consent or handoff boundary
- the evidence, rollback, retention, or obsolescence route
- whether the output remains a technique, becomes a skill proposal, or should
  stay as a mechanics receipt

If those cannot be named, keep the material in Method-growth rather than
promoting or activating it.
