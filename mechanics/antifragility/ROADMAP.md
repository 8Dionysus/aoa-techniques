# Antifragility Roadmap

This roadmap records future contour for the `aoa-techniques` antifragility
package. It does not change technique status, health status, proof status,
generated contracts, or validator behavior by itself.

## Current Contour

Antifragility is the local route for stress, chaos, degraded-mode, and
recovery-oriented practice pressure that may become one portable technique.

- `chaos-wave-program` is the local home for bounded chaos and stress program
  practice distilled from the pre-split donor-wave surface.
- `recovery-practice-bridge` is the local home for bridges between
  antifragility pressure and current recovery or validation techniques.
- Antifragility pressure is currently non-ORQ candidate-only pressure in this
  repo, not a direct AoA owner-request landing.

## Next Work

1. Keep antifragility references pointed at part-local homes as new stress,
   degraded-mode, recovery, or failure-analysis candidates appear.
2. Add a new part only if repeated technique-layer antifragility signals no
   longer fit the chaos wave program or recovery practice bridge.
3. Promote a reusable antifragility practice into `techniques/` only after it
   can name one atomic move, likely domain, likely kind, family posture,
   capability, substrate, execution profile, risk posture, relations, and
   validation.
4. Keep donor-wave details in `legacy/raw/` unless an active part needs a
   distilled route.

## When Time Comes

- Add public-safe stress examples only when they come from reviewed local
  evidence.
- Route proof-strength claims to `aoa-evals`, runtime repair to the runtime
  owner, and memory or stats interpretation to their owning repos.
- Reassess this roadmap after an antifragility candidate is promoted or after
  repeated recovery practice no longer fits existing technique bundles.

## Out Of Scope

- one-score health claims.
- deletion as performance theater.
- owner-local cleanup authority, deletion approval, or repair acceptance.
- proof verdicts, public repair proof, runtime self-healing, memory truth,
  stats meaning, playbook choreography, routing truth, or automatic technique promotion.
