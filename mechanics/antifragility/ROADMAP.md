# Antifragility Roadmap

This roadmap records future contour for the `aoa-techniques` antifragility
package. It does not change technique status, health status, proof status,
generated contracts, or validator behavior by itself.

## Current State

- `chaos-wave-program` is the local home for bounded chaos and stress program
  practice distilled from the pre-split donor-wave surface.
- `recovery-practice-bridge` is the local home for bridges between antifragility
  pressure and current recovery or validation techniques.
- Antifragility pressure is currently non-ORQ candidate-only pressure in this
  repo, not a direct AoA owner-request landing.

## Next Honest Moves

- Keep antifragility references pointed at part-local homes as new stress,
  degraded-mode, recovery, or failure-analysis candidates appear.
- Add a new part only if repeated technique-layer antifragility signals no
  longer fit the chaos wave program or recovery practice bridge.
- Promote a reusable antifragility practice into `techniques/` only after it can
  name one atomic move, likely domain, likely kind, family posture, capability,
  substrate, execution profile, risk posture, relations, and validation.
- Keep donor-wave details in `legacy/raw/` unless an active part needs a
  distilled route.

## Stop-lines

- Do not make one-score health claims from this package.
- Do not turn deletion into performance theater.
- Do not claim owner-local cleanup authority, deletion approval, or repair
  acceptance.
- Do not import proof verdicts, public repair proof, runtime self-healing,
  memory truth, stats meaning, playbook choreography, routing truth, or
  automatic technique promotion into this package.
