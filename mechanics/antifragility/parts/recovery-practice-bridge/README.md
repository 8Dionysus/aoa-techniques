# Recovery Practice Bridge

Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose

This part connects antifragility pressure to current recovery and validation
technique bundles without changing bundle status.

It belongs to the local antifragility mechanic as a bridge between stress
signals and reusable technique canon, not as proof, public health authority, or
automatic promotion.

## Owns

- technique-anchor references
- antifragility-recovery family posture
- candidate-to-technique review route
- stop-lines before proof or runtime authority

## Technique Anchors

| Technique | Current role |
|---|---|
| `AOA-T-0097` `degrade-reground-recover` | Degraded continuation and regrounding practice. |
| `AOA-T-0098` `receipt-first-failure-analysis` | Failure review from owner-local receipts. |
| `AOA-T-0099` `isolated-service-stop-on-shared-substrate` | Bounded runtime stop without widening into full teardown. |
| `AOA-T-0100` `stress-receipt-reground-closeout` | Bounded stress receipt, regrounding, owner route, and reviewed closeout. |

The family posture is `antifragility-recovery`: degraded continuation,
regrounding, and receipt-first failure analysis.

Bundle meaning, status, and validation strength remain in
`techniques/**/TECHNIQUE.md`, not in this bridge.

## Must Not Do

- promote or demote technique bundles
- certify repair success
- compute proof verdicts
- become a health score
- replace owner-local receipts
- route runtime self-healing

## Review Gate

Before a new antifragility practice becomes a technique bundle, it must name:

- one atomic move
- likely `domain` and primary `kind`
- likely family or reason no family is stable yet
- capability class, substrate, execution profile, and risk posture
- nearest related techniques, alternatives, or conflict points
- owner boundary, portable core, and stop-line

## Exit Signal

This surface is ready to produce a new technique candidate only when repeated
stress-recovery evidence shows one portable move that is smaller than a playbook,
weaker than proof, and independent of AoA runtime topology.
