## Status

Accepted.

## Context

`AOA-T-0085 multi-axis-quest-overlay` was originally classified as
`artifact`, but the first kind ambiguity direct-read review found that its
center of gravity is not a primary durable artifact shape.

The bundle adds a quest-, RPG-, or chronicle-shaped overlay on top of reviewed
progression or route evidence. That overlay must stay adjunct and weaker than
the owner, proof, and routing surfaces underneath it.

## Decision

Remap `AOA-T-0085` from `artifact` to `lift`.

Keep its `domain`, `status`, ID, evidence, relations, and promoted posture
unchanged. The change is a classification correction only.

## Alternatives

- Keep `artifact`.
  Rejected because it overstates the overlay as a primary durable shape when
  the bundle repeatedly says the overlay rests on stronger reviewed evidence.
- Add a new kind such as `overlay`.
  Rejected because the current registry already has the right tie-break:
  derived surfaces from stronger source material belong in `lift`.
- Move the bundle out of `agent-workflows`.
  Rejected because the reusable object remains a workflow-side reflective
  overlay, not docs canon, proof doctrine, route policy, or memory semantics.

## Consequences

- Generated kind surfaces will list `AOA-T-0085` under `lift`.
- The Distillation reform shortlist moves to `AOA-T-0005` next.
- `AOA-T-0085` remains `promoted`; no canonical claim or status change is made.
- Future remaps should keep using direct bundle reading before frontmatter
  changes.
